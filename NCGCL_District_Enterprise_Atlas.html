Comparison within the province

Definitions, coverage and source evidence

The district architecture retains the 135 four-province Economic Census systems and adds Islamabad as a satellite. It is not a claim about the present number of administrative districts. Population matches 136 systems; agriculture matches 136 source names; rural Mouza data match 127. Cholistan remains separate in the native agricultural table.

Establishment density = establishments / resident population × 1,000. Manufacturing specialization (LQ) = district manufacturing share / national manufacturing share. Neither statistic measures productivity. Composition groups describe standardized scale and sector shares.

The broad lower size category includes establishments where workforce was not mentioned. District totals can reconcile while sector-size cells disagree; construction size inference is withheld. Farm-band area/count inconsistencies remain unresolved in the national report.

Evidence grades: A directly observed, B derived, C statistically estimated, D modelled, E inferred, F externally benchmarked, G illustrative, H unresolved. The economic and population controls use A/B, farm and labour estimates C, and opportunity screens E.

Sources: PBS Economic Census 2023–24 and Population Census 2023; Agricultural Census 2024 detailed source tables; Mouza Census 2020 supplied SPSS file. Full source names, revisions and hashes are provided in the confidence workbook and technical annex.
