Pakistan Crop Insurance / AYII · Government programme design

From policy choice to beneficiaries, pricing, fiscal cost and payout capacity.

Build a programme by geography, farm size, crop, insured value and subsidy. The calculator separates beneficiary targeting from crop exposure, applies V12.5 actuarial anchors and district-tier prices, and shows the resulting national or sub-national portfolio.

Core rule

The controlled V12.5 product is an 80% AYII trigger. Alternative triggers are diagnostic. Commercial loads are kept separate from the technical risk price and default to zero until quoted.

04 · Model logic

What is hard data, what is actuarial output, and what is a planning assumption

The calculator is deliberately explicit about data lineage so that a fiscal decision cannot be mistaken for an actuarial result.

A. Beneficiary counts

2024 Agricultural Census current farm counts by size band are used for national and province selection. District beneficiaries are allocated as a planning proxy because the district atlas still carries source-vintage farm counts.

B. Crop acreage

Major-crop shares by farm-size band are applied to cropped area. This allows the farm-size policy filter to change the exposure base rather than simply changing the number of beneficiaries.

C. Pricing methodology

Four selectable methods are shown on the same exposure base: V12.5 80% AYII; CLIS+ deep-catastrophe pricing at a 50% tehsil-yield threshold; IAP proposed crop premiums; and a flat 2% benchmark. District differentiation applies to V12.5 and is preserved proportionally for CLIS+.

D. Sums insured

Default SI / acre: wheat 100,859; rice 125,000; spring maize 115,000; autumn maize 105,000; cotton 135,000. The insured-value slider scales these amounts without changing the yield trigger.

E. Support

The default affordability bridge applies the V12.5 farmer contribution formula after gross pricing. A policymaker can switch to a fixed farmer or government percentage without altering the technical price.

F. Payout cap

The calculator shows the lower of the contract maximum (policy payout % of SI) and the selected aggregate premium multiple. This makes the difference between a per-policy indemnity cap and a portfolio-loss cap visible.

G. Historical replay

For 2005-06 to 2024-25, current selected exposure is held constant and multiplied by the controlled national crop-year 80% AYII loss series. This isolates historical severity. CLIS+ is flagged as a non-comparable 50% trigger until a controlled 50% replay is loaded.

Gross premium = Σ (insured acres × SI/acre × insured-value % × selected pricing rate) + applicable commercial loads
Farmer premium (default) = min[gross, 3% of SI + 25% of premium above 3%, 15% of expected profit]; public support = gross − farmer premium
