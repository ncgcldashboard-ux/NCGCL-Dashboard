1

The NPL stock is not a default probability

Default flows are inferred under an explicit residence-time grid (2-4 years). Corporate TTC 4.2%, SME 6.5%, agriculture 6.5%; negative stock movements are diagnostics, never PD.

2

Two structural breaks are booked as adjustments, not defaults

Dec-2024 SBP coverage expansion (financing +26% to +40% in a quarter) and the Sep-2024 SME NPL step (+45bn, absent from the separate SME file). Industry series are truncated at Jun-2024.

3

LGD is economic, evidence-anchored and priced at a policy floor

System economic LGD 43%-54% (SBP flows / bank exit mix), anchor 48.5%, downturn 52%; 16 security types are calibrated to the anchor and the 62.6% cash-only bound is the pricing floor. At the floor, expected loss is 5.9x observed credit cost (2.0x on nominal LGD): the difference is time value already inside the economic LGD (decision D8).

4

Macro sensitivity cannot be read from NPL flows

Regressions of default flows on macro stress have the wrong sign in 2016-24 (forbearance, restructuring, nominal growth). PIT / stress sensitivities are volatility-scaled priors (class C); severe PDs are floored at 2.2x TTC.

5

Policy capital binds in 3 of 7 facilities - economic capital binds in the agriculture and first-loss tails

Portfolio EC 99.9% PKR 1,576m vs policy capital PKR 1,102m (economic capital binds for the portfolio). Required fees are a multiple of proposed fees: the gap is an explicit concession to size and source.

6

Second-loss is a tail-dependence bet

Same pool triggers a second-loss claim payment in 1.5% of Gaussian paths and 5.2% of Student-t paths (net loss above the attachment: 0.4% and 2.9%): price with a model-uncertainty charge and manual approval.

7

Eight risk grades, a PD for each exposure type

Grade 1 (lowest risk) to 8; grade 5 = 4.5% for a listed corporate, 7.0% for an unlisted SME, 7.0% for agriculture, 1.0% for a partner institution; D = default. Single names are priced on the grade PD; programmes keep their pool PD.

8

PACRA and VIS calibrate migration, not default levels

PACRA: no default after CY16 in 2,236 obligor-years; VIS: 9 in 2,061. Migration is fitted on the eight grades with default absorbing; it moves 5-year PD by at most 6%. Rated names default less than the ladder implies (24 defaults expected for PACRA, none observed): agency labels are ordinal only.

9

The macro state uses the policy-rate, CPI and 3M KIBOR files

All eleven recalled policy rates verified; 2026 nowcast: CPI 11.1% (Aug-2026), 3M KIBOR 11.7%, real KIBOR 0.3%; PIT PDs 2%-8% above the last full year.

10

SME risk: a scorecard that ranks, a PD level that stays on the anchor, and a priced correlation range

The SME-native scorecard (nine observable factors, expert priors, class C) ranks unlisted borrowers; partner-institution default history enters programme PDs by credibility; SME pools are re-priced under correlation x0.5 / x1.5 and Student-t. The dependence range sets the model-uncertainty charge at 2.01% for the pari passu pool and 5.23% for the second-loss layer; the tail weight is the largest swing factor (decision D12).

What this model is for

Catalytic finance: limited risk capital, priced and shared through structured guarantees so that PFIs can lend into SME, agriculture and climate segments they avoid

NCGCL guarantees PFI lending as a third-party guarantor. The model answers five sequential questions for every proposed guarantee: (1) the underlying risk of the obligor or pool, (2) what can be recovered, (3) what the partner institution adds, (4) how the contract turns losses into NCGCL cash claims, and (5) whether expected and tail loss fit risk appetite and earn the required return. Borrower, bank and facility scores are never averaged: they describe different failure channels.
