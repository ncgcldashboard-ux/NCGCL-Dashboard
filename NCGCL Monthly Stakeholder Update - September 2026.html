Executive Snapshot

Financial & Investment data as at September 30, 2026 · Guarantee exposure per the September management accounts · Deal Pipeline, Products and Collaborations refreshed from the Guarantee Origination & Tracker

Guarantee Book Grows to PKR 830.7 Mn — Exposure Reconciled, Up PKR 320.7 Mn on the Last Confirmed Figure
Outstanding guarantee exposure reached PKR 830.7 Mn at 30 September 2026, against the PKR 510 Mn held in the August update pending reconciliation. Two new guarantees were issued on 3 September — RCDP via PMIC (PKR 250 Mn) and SAFCO via HBL FI (PKR 40 Mn) — and the Karandaaz / Samarkar KCBL guarantee (PKR 30.7 Mn, outstanding since June) is now disclosed. The management accounts and the Deal Tracker agree on PMIC (PKR 500 Mn) and HBL FI (PKR 300 Mn). Sanctioned limits are unchanged at PKR 11,737.5 Mn across 9 counterparties; utilisation of the sanctioned register rises to 6.8% (from 4.3%). See Section C.
Financial Performance — PKR 320.5 Mn YTD Net Profit, 14% Above Budget · First Full Month Without Debt
YTD net profit of PKR 320.5 Mn is 14% above the internal budget (PKR 280.5 Mn) and within 1% of prorated FY2025 (PKR 324.3 Mn); the gap to prior year has narrowed from −5% (July) and −2% (August) to −1%. September on its own contributed PKR 38.8 Mn of net profit on PKR 76.8 Mn of other income. Finance cost for the month was only PKR 0.8 Mn (lease interest), confirming the running finance facility settled in August no longer weighs on earnings. Guarantee revenue reached PKR 1.6 Mn YTD against a PKR 21.1 Mn budget.
Investments — 11.68% MTD Yield, +92 bps Alpha, Ranked 2nd Among Peer Funds
The portfolio returned 11.68% MTD against a 10.76% benchmark (+92 bps) and 10.62% YTD against 10.40% (+22 bps). Under a wait-and-watch stance as the yield curve repriced 47–82 bps in the 2–20 year segment, no long-tenor paper was bought; PKR 400 Mn was placed in Alfalah Cash Fund-II on 30 September at an indicative 11.75–11.85%.
